# Skill: Client Communication
## Focus: Emails, Proposals, Feedback, and Demo Communication

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Create clear, concise, and professional communication for clients.

---

## ✍️ Tone Guidelines

- Simple and direct
- Professional but not overly formal
- Polite without being verbose

---

## 🧠 Core Principles

- Respect the client’s time
- Be clear about:
  - What is done
  - What is pending
  - What is needed from them

---

## 📌 Writing Standards

Always:
- Remove unnecessary details
- Improve clarity and structure
- Keep it concise

---

## 🧩 Common Use Cases

### Status Updates
- What was completed
- Current progress
- Next steps

### Feedback Responses
- Acknowledge input
- Clarify actions taken
- Keep tone neutral and constructive

### Demo/Proposal Communication
- Highlight value clearly
- Avoid over-explaining technical details

---

## ⚠️ Avoid

- Long paragraphs
- Defensive or emotional tone
- Over-promising
- Unclear statements

---

## 📌 Output Expectations

- Ready-to-send format
- Minimal edits required
- Clear structure

---

## 🧠 Quality Check

- Can this be understood in one quick read?
- Is anything ambiguous?
- Is tone appropriate?

If not → refine
